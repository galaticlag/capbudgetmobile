CapBudget

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-18

CapBudget is a local-first budgeting app. The short version: nothing leaves your device, and we collect nothing.

What data the app handles

Everything you put into CapBudget — bank transactions, account references, categories, categorization rules, budget targets, and app settings — is stored only in a single SQLite database on your device (household.sqlite), in the app's own private storage. There is no account, no login, and no CapBudget server.

What we collect

Nothing. No analytics or usage tracking, no advertising or advertising identifiers, no crash or diagnostics reporting, no third-party SDKs that phone home, no network calls to any CapBudget-operated service (there is none). The app functions fully offline.

CSV imports

When you import a bank CSV export, the file is parsed on your device. It is not uploaded anywhere and is not retained by the app unless you choose “keep” during import.

Backups

You can export a backup from Settings. The backup is a file you control — CapBudget does not upload it and has no access to wherever you save it.

Permissions

The app requests no location, contacts, camera, microphone, or other sensitive permissions — only its own private storage.

Optional tips

CapBudget is free. If you wish, you can leave the developer an optional tip; a tip never unlocks anything. On iOS this uses Apple’s in-app purchase: the payment is processed entirely by Apple, and CapBudget never sees your payment details, your Apple ID or your purchase history, and receives no personal information. On Android and in a web browser a button opens an external donation page in your browser; that page is operated by a third party (Buy Me a Coffee) under its own privacy policy, and nothing from the app is sent to it.

Bank-account references

An account number/IBAN from your CSV is stored to group transactions by account, kept only on your device. In the UI only the last 4 characters are shown.

Children

CapBudget is a general-purpose finance tool and is not directed at children.
